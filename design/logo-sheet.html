Identity · v1

A hand that has left the page

A cursive ascender loop, lifted clear of the line it was written on. The gap between stroke and baseline is the alpha channel, drawn — and it spells the name: ink, lifted. The self-crossing is what makes it read as a hand rather than a logo. Not a pen: a pen is an input device, and this product is about output.

01

The mark

Geometry comes from the 16px tray grid and scales up, never the other way round — baseline y13–15, stroke (4,10)→(13,3), gap 3px, ×32. It is two filled shapes and one colour, so it survives macOS template mode, GNOME symbolic and XFCE's near-1-bit rendering unchanged.

03

Wordmark

Your references all share one move: a letter doing double duty, and a detached element — the nib's drop, the descender's drip. Ours inverts it. Theirs falls; ours lifts. "ink" stays on the rule; "lift" has come off it, carrying the contact shadow. The word does what it says.

04

The icon as an object

Generated with Higgsfield from the vector mark as reference — a slab of frosted optical glass in Prussian blue with the ink suspended inside it, casting a real shadow onto the back face. The visual system's whole thesis made physical: the instrument is glass, the specimen is ink. For marketing and the About screen, never as the shipped icon — that stays vector.

05

Rules

  • Clear space equals the height of the baseline bar — h/8 of the mark — on all four sides. Nothing enters it.
  • Minimum sizes. Mark 16px. Wordmark 17px. Below that, the mark alone.
  • Never close the gap. It is the whole idea. Never centre the stroke over the baseline, never let a shadow bridge it.
  • Never add a pen, nib, quill or cursor. Those are input devices; this product is about output. hard rule
  • The contact shadow is optional but the gap is not. Drop the shadow in one-colour contexts — etching, embroidery, a single-bit tray — and the mark still reads.
  • Colour: Prussian #2B6486 on light, #79B4DC on dark, or one flat colour. Iron Gall #8B5E34 is for display type only, never the mark.
  • Do not rotate, outline, gradient-fill or add a stroke to the mark. It is two filled shapes.
